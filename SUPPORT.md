## Response Times

KiloCenter Community Edition is community-supported. There is no guaranteed response time or SLA. Maintainers review issues and pull requests as capacity allows.

## Commercial Support

For production deployments with SLA-backed support, multi-tenancy, and enterprise MIOTY features, see [kiloiot.io](https://kiloiot.io/mioty-service-center/).
